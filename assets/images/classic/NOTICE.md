# Windows 98 icons

User-selected collection: Windows 98 Icon Viewer, hosted by Alex Meub.
https://win98icons.alexmeub.com/

Five native 32x32 PNG files in icons/32/ provide the section-heading icons,
including the tree for About me, displayed at their original size.
Eight original 48x48 PNG files remain in icons/ as retained source assets;
none are rendered in the classic variant.
Seven native 16x16 PNG files in icons/16/ provide the contact/profile/CV icons,
displayed at their original size. The 16x16 and 32x32 variants were extracted unchanged
from the collection's official "Download All Icons" ZIP; they were not resized
or converted from the larger artwork. Source URLs, archive entries, intrinsic
dimensions, and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in manifest.json. The numeric
filename suffix does not identify pixel size.

The collection was explicitly selected by the user for this variant.
No explicit artwork reuse license was found on the viewer page; no MIT or
other open-source artwork license is claimed here. Credit the collection
and retain these provenance notes with redistributed resources.
